# Hi, I'm Bruno

**I like making things that people can feel: code, sound, art and everything in between.**

Backend developer in digital health, doing a Master's in Digital Game Development, and making music on the side. Most interested in level design, game feel and atmosphere. Dark Souls is the reason.
My strength is connecting disciplines: I speak programmer, designer, editor and musician, and I care a lot about polish, about the difference between something that works and something that feels finished.

### Things I've built

- **Asylum Needed**: a C++ terminal roguelike. You play a psychiatric patient whose illness is your power, escaping a horror hospital. Turn-based combat, levelling, and procedurally generated dungeons. *(repo coming soon)*
- **Drone swarm connectivity** (bachelor's project): a C++ module that moves and rotates a drone to align its Starlink Mini with the satellite for the strongest signal, part of a rescue-drone swarm.
- **Snake AI with limited vision** (bachelor's AI course): an agent that plays Snake without seeing the whole board.
